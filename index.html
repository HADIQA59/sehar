01 — Dua

Aap Ke Liye

Ek saal aur guzar gaya, aur aap har guzarte din ke saath aur behtar hotay ja rahe hain. Yeh saal aap ke liye sukoon lekar aaye, mehnat ka sila kamyabi ki soorat mein mile, aur har mushkil raasta khud ba khud aasan ho jaye.

Janamdin bohat bohat mubarak ho.

02 — Khaas Baatein

Chand Baatein

  • Aap ki wajah se mushkil raaste bhi aasan lagte hain.
  • Aap ko chhoti chhoti baatein bhi yaad rehti hain.
  • Aap ka sukoon har mahol ko behtar bana deta hai.
  • Aap bina kahay hamesha saath khare hotay hain.

03 — Khat

Aap Ke Naam Ek Khat

Kuch log zindagi mein sirf shamil nahi hotay, woh usay behtar bana detay hain. Aap unhi mein se hain. Is saal jo bhi raste banein, main chahta hoon aap unhein apni shart par, apni raftaar se tay karein — aur main yeh sab dekhne ke liye hamesha saath rahunga.

— Dil se

06 — Shukriya

Shukriya

Har us waqt ke liye shukriya jab aap ne baat sunii bina kahay, har us madad ke liye jo bina maangay mili. Aap ka hona hi bohat kuch aasan bana deta hai.
